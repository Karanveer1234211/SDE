# BigMove S02/V3 negative-SDE frozen candidate — execution research

## Research contract
- Original seven setup signals are imported from the CONSO v2 exact-freeze event file. No frozen rules are modified.
- S02/V3/SDE are measured at the **completed signal close**. Entry on that same close is NOT assumed executable.
- Delay 1: next-session open; delay 2: following-session open. Horizon 3/5 counts entry session as day 1.
- Stops/targets evaluated from entry-session OHLC. Gap stops/targets fill at opening price. Both-hit bars use separate pessimistic/optimistic paths.
- MAE/MFE use full daily bar extremes, including the exit bar; extrema order is unknown. This can overstate pre-exit excursions.
- ATR stop uses 14-period simple true-range ATR at signal close; not Wilder ATR.
- 0.35 percentage-point round-trip cost; no extra slippage, circuit limits, spread or impact.
- The realized-exit portfolio proxy is **not** mark-to-market and does not establish portfolio viability.
- Strategies evaluated on the SAME historical data that discovered S02/V3/SDE are exploratory, NOT out-of-sample.
- Unmatched signal dates: 0; nonconsecutive original entry dates: 0.
